# Time to a usable snapshot through bench/replay, 2026-10-15

Recorded pages served from localhost by `bench/replay` (no live network: no DNS, TLS handshakes with servers, CDN or server latency).
Per page and tool 5 fresh-process runs with empty caches: Sweef = `sweef open` then `sweef snapshot`, repeated every 250 ms until the snapshot holds the page's expected text; Chromium 141 = fresh launch, `goto(load)`, Playwright's AI snapshot, repeated the same way. Median (min..max) over passing runs, ms. Host: 4 vCPU Xeon @ 2.1 GHz.
`before` = Sweef at dca9bbf (the start of this work), `after` = worktree-gaps HEAD. Script: `bench/realsites/node/replay_table.mjs`; data: `replay-2026-10-15{,-before}.json`.
